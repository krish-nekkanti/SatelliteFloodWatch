DATA & ATTRIBUTION

Sources

Satellite Floodwatch combines a mapped flood-hazard layer with terrain, weather, river, alert, and news data. Each source is listed below with how it is used. Open a source for its own data and technical details.

FLOOD HAZARD MODEL

Global 100-year river-flood hazard

This ArcGIS MapServer provides the global modeled flood-hazard tiles displayed in the Flood Map. The map is intended for broad exploration and preliminary planning, not parcel-level decisions or emergency use.

Used for: the flood-hazard overlay and the "in a mapped flood zone?" check.

OPEN ARCGIS MAP SERVICE ↗
SATELLITE IMAGERY

Esri World Imagery

Basemap imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS user community.

Used for: the satellite basemap on the main map and the rainfall mini-map.

OPEN ESRI SERVICE ↗
TERRAIN

ASTER Global Digital Elevation Model

A 30 m elevation model produced from NASA's Terra satellite and Japan's METI, served through OpenTopoData. A global terrain dataset is used as a fallback where ASTER has no value. Elevation error is roughly ±10 m, and the data is a surface model, so trees and buildings can be included.

Used for: ground elevation and the low-lying terrain check.

OPEN OPENTOPODATA ↗
OBSERVED RAINFALL

NASA POWER

Daily precipitation derived from NASA satellite and reanalysis data. Observations lag real time by several days.

Used for: recent observed rainfall.

OPEN NASA POWER ↗
WEATHER FORECAST

Open-Meteo Forecast API

Open-weather-model forecasts of daily precipitation. Forecasts are less certain beyond about three days.

Used for: rain over the past 3 and next 7 days, and the 7-day outlook.

OPEN OPEN-METEO ↗
RIVER DISCHARGE

GloFAS via the Open-Meteo Flood API

The Global Flood Awareness System (Copernicus / ECMWF) models river discharge at about 5 km resolution. Small streams may not be represented.

Used for: river flow compared with normal, now and in the forecast.

OPEN GLOFAS ↗
OFFICIAL ALERTS (US)

US National Weather Service

Active flood warnings, watches, and advisories for the exact point selected. Coverage is limited to the United States.

Used for: official flood alerts. An active warning forces a HIGH status.

OPEN NWS API ↗
FLOOD & CYCLONE EVENTS

GDACS

The Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System, a UN and European Commission framework, lists flood events and tropical cyclones with alert levels.

Used for: recent flood events within 300 km and the storm list.

OPEN GDACS ↗
SATELLITE RAINFALL MAP

NASA GPM IMERG via NASA GIBS

Near-real-time satellite precipitation estimates delivered through NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services. Data typically runs several hours behind real time.

Used for: the rainfall layer in the Rainfall & Storms mini-map.

OPEN NASA GIBS ↗
NEWS HEADLINES

GDELT Project

GDELT indexes news from around the world. floodwatch shows flood-related headlines, links to the original publishers, and labels articles from a vetted list of outlets. Other outlets are marked as not verified. Headlines are context only and are not part of the risk score.

Used for: the Recent Flood Reporting panel.

OPEN GDELT ↗
PLACE SEARCH

OpenStreetMap Nominatim

Place search and reverse geocoding. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

Used for: searching locations and naming clicked points.

Leaflet

An open-source JavaScript library for interactive maps.

Used for: both maps on the Flood Map page.
