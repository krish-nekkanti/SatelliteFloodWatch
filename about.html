ABOUT SATELLITE FLOODWATCH

Easy to Understand Information About Floods.

Satellite Floodwatch is a global flood monitoring project that uses satellite data to provide real-time information about flooding around the world.

Image from the Recent Nepal Floods
01 / PURPOSE

Provide important data

The map helps people understand the risks of flooding in their area, allowing them to make informed decisions about where to live and work. Provides data such as elevation, risk of flooding, and necessary elevation for buildings.

02 / DATA

Start with the source.

The map displays data take from NASA Satellites, and other sources and is displayed clearly on an interactive map.

03 / LIMITS

Do NOT use this Website for emergency situations.

This website is NOT a substitute for emergency flood services. It is intended for casual use cases and preliminary planning. During any emergency PLEASE follow the instructions of local emergency services. This was made in a day, so take any information with a grain of salt.
